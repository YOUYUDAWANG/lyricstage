# Performance direction anti-patterns

- Reject a background transformation justified only by one lyric keyword.
- Reject Hero when the target line is too long to remain complete or when the previous Hero has not had a stable section between them.
- Reject a duet layout inferred from pronouns, punctuation, or translated meaning; require voice roles or real overlap.
- Reject an effect that cites line indices outside its section, unknown triggers, duplicate primitives, conflicting primitives, more than two support layers, or total cost above six.
- Reject rapid unrelated art-direction changes. Section development must remain recognizably inside the song motif.
- Reject decorative motion that delays word reveal, simulates timing absent from the source, or makes previous/next context unreadable.
- Prefer no effect and complete three-level Reading when evidence is weak.
- Reject empty translucent rectangles that resemble UI panels but contain no content or semantic function.
- Reject default sci-fi styling: persistent grids, rails, scanning lines, symmetric pillars, converging rays and particles cannot be used as generic atmosphere.
- Reject duplicate structure across environment and lyric layers. A section receives at most one structural motif owner.
- Reject a palette unrelated to the cover. The runtime owns deterministic artwork extraction; direction should use palette roles rather than inventing raw colors.
- Reject continuous high-salience geometry during intimate, reflective or reassuring Reading passages; prefer color, light, material and negative space.
